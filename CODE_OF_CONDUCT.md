# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Vertex Velocity Web Panel to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish other people's personal information.
- Follow the project's license and attribution requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or abusive conduct.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code or misleading security claims.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be sent privately through the official repository's available contact or reporting channels.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project publicly.
